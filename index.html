Franchise Opportunity Profile

Maaco

Maaco is an automobile painting and body repair franchise founded in 1972. Every fact on this page is sourced from the MAACO FRANCHISOR SPV LLC 2025 Franchise Disclosure Document (with December 2025 amendment), with Item and page citations.

Key Facts

Initial Franchise Fee

$45,000

Non-refundable. $22,500 for a second center, $20,000 for the third and each subsequent center. (FDD Item 5, p.22)

Estimated Initial Investment

$196,000 to $644,000

Auto Body Conversion model. Ground Up or Non-Auto Retrofit: $728,500 to $3,994,000. (FDD Item 7, pp.30-32)

Royalty

8% of Gross Receipts

Payable weekly. Newly developed centers pay 4% for the first 6 months. (FDD Item 6, p.25)

Marketing Fee

$1,200 / week

Or the weekly marketing budget of franchisees in the designated market area, whichever is greater — in addition to the $15,000 Initial Advertising Contribution. (FDD Item 6, p.25)

Territory

Non-exclusive

No exclusive territory. Density protection: no more than one center per 50,000 people in the market's Core Based Statistical Area (if in compliance). (FDD Item 12, p.48)

Term

15 years + 15-year renewal

Renewal requires good standing, refurbishment, a $2,500 renewal fee, and signing the then-current agreement. (FDD Item 17, p.56)

Training

127 classroom hours

In Charlotte, NC, under the COO; covers sales, CCC One estimating/POS, production, KPIs, and a final success plan. $10,000 training & opening fee. (FDD Item 11, p.47)

Owner Model

Full-time owner-operator

The owner or approved principal operator must devote full time, energy, and efforts to managing the center. (FDD Item 15, p.53)

Financing

None offered

The franchisor offers no direct or indirect financing and guarantees no note, lease, or obligation. (FDD Item 10, p.39)

Software

$799–$973 / month

CCC One Base Package license (stepping schedule through 2029), plus taxes; collected by the franchisor. (FDD Item 6, pp.25-26)

HQ

Charlotte, North Carolina

MAACO FRANCHISOR SPV LLC (Delaware LLC), 440 South Church Street, Suite 700, Charlotte, NC 28202. (704) 377-8855. (FDD Item 1, p.1)

System Size

363 outlets

All franchised, 0 company-owned, as of 12/28/2024; net change −13 in 2024, −22 in 2023. (FDD Item 20, p.69)

Item 19 — Financial Performance

Measurement period: fiscal year December 31, 2023 through December 28, 2024. 317 franchised centers qualified (open at least 2 full years, full-production locations, full-year reported data) — about 87% of the 363 centers operating at year-end; average 25.9 years in operation.

  • Average Gross Receipts (n=317): $1,615,904 · Median: $1,348,304
  • Top 50% average: $2,226,532 (range $1,348,304 – $10,463,135) · Bottom 50% average: $1,001,411 (range $445,821 – $1,342,652)
  • 110 of 317 centers (~35%) met or exceeded the average gross receipts
  • Key indicators (averages): Trade 36% · Conversion 53% · Retention 50% · Estimates 1,102 · Total cars 869
  • Confidence: STRONG — n=317 centers, 87% of the system, reporting average/median/high/low for gross receipts plus operating KPIs.

Gross receipts means cash collected plus work completed but unpaid for 90 days, less sales taxes. Excluded: 11 centers open fewer than 2 full years, 33 centers without full-year reports, 2 satellite locations, and 19 centers that closed during the fiscal year. Some outlets have achieved these results; individual results may differ; there is no assurance you will do as well. (FDD Item 19, pp.64–68.)

Risk Profile & Disclosure

49 / 100 risk score

Assessment: Moderate. A 50+ year-old system with strong Item 19 reporting (317 centers, average $1.6M gross receipts), tempered by two pending franchisee-vs-franchisor lawsuits seeking over $1 million each plus treble damages, a non-exclusive territory with only density-based protection, 8% royalties plus a $1,200 weekly marketing fee, a full-time owner-operator requirement, no franchisor financing, and a net decline of 48 outlets from 2022 to 2024.

Risk inputs (documented):

  • Capital intensity: dual-model; Ground Up / Non-Auto Retrofit ceiling $3,994,000 — pushes score up
  • Litigation: 2 pending franchisee-vs-franchisor suits (ad-fund misappropriation claims, filed 11/2025; fraud/DTPA claims, transferred to W.D.N.C.) — pushes score up
  • Territory: non-exclusive; only CBSA density protection (1 center per 50,000 persons) — pushes score up
  • Owner-operator model: full-time devotion to management required (Item 15) — pushes score up
  • No direct or indirect financing offered (Item 10) — pushes score up
  • Net unit decline 2022–2024: −48 outlets (−11.7%) — pushes score up
  • Renewal cost drag: $2,500 fee + signing then-current agreement with potentially different terms — pushes score up
  • Item 19 strength: 317 centers (~87% of system), average gross receipts $1,615,904, median $1,348,304 — pushes score down
  • Audited financial statements attached (Exhibit A) with Driven Systems performance guaranty — pushes score down
  • Driven Brands platform backing (indirect parent) — pushes score down
